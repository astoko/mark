# Salamander Grand Piano V3

Recorded Yamaha C5 grand piano by **Alexander Holm**, licensed under
[Creative Commons Attribution 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/).

These 30 MP3 files (one every minor third, A0–C8) are the web-optimized subset
distributed by the Tone.js project (https://tonejs.github.io/audio/salamander/).
Original: https://archive.org/details/SalamanderGrandPianoV3
